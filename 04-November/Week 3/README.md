### 2. Read of Martins et al. — 2 h

Slide-level classification of LBC WSIs from **1,235 patients** of the A.C.Camargo Cancer Center using only the labels of the pathology reports: tiles go through a frozen feature extractor and a MIL aggregator.

### 3. Read of Ilse et al. — 2 h

The paper that introduces **ABMIL**: the bag representation is a weighted average of the instance embeddings, with weights given by a (gated) attention network trained end-to-end, which makes the model permutation-invariant and also shows which instances drive the bag decision.
